About the founder

Karunya Mudidana

Counselling Psychologist | Behavioural Therapist

Karunya promotes mental well-being through evidence-based, client-centred care. She believes every individual deserves a safe, non-judgmental space to express their emotions, overcome challenges, and build resilience.

About MindVerse

MindVerse supports individuals through emotional, behavioural, and psychological challenges, all in a safe, confidential online setting.

Our goal is to help you understand yourself better, develop healthy coping skills, improve your emotional well-being, and lead a balanced and meaningful life.

Karunya's approach focuses on understanding each person's unique experiences and empowering them with practical strategies to improve their mental health and overall quality of life.
